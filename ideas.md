## Idea 1: "Coastal Prestige" — Nautical Modernism

**Design Movement:** Coastal Modernism meets Corporate Prestige

**Core Principles:**

1. Deep navy and ocean teal as primary palette — evoking UCSB's coastal identity
2. Serif display headings (Playfair Display) paired with clean sans-serif body (DM Sans)
3. Asymmetric layouts with bold typographic anchors
4. Generous whitespace punctuated by full-bleed imagery

**Color Philosophy:**

- Primary: Deep Navy `#0A1628`
- Accent: Ocean Teal `#1A7A8A`
- Highlight: Sandy Gold `#C9A84C`
- Background: Off-White `#F8F6F2`
- Text: Charcoal `#2C2C2C`
  Emotional intent: Conveys prestige, coastal identity, and entrepreneurial ambition.

**Layout Paradigm:**

- Sticky translucent nav with blur effect
- Full-viewport hero with parallax scroll
- Stats section with large numerals and thin dividers
- Staggered two-column values section
- Horizontal scrolling company logos ticker

**Signature Elements:**

1. Wave-inspired SVG dividers between sections
2. Gold underline accents on headings
3. Circular member photo frames with navy borders

**Interaction Philosophy:**

- Smooth scroll with section fade-ins
- Hover states reveal gold accent underlines
- Button hover: fill from bottom

**Animation:**

- Hero text: staggered word-by-word entrance
- Stats: count-up animation on scroll
- Section content: slide-up fade-in on viewport entry

**Typography System:**

- Display: Playfair Display Bold (headings)
- Body: DM Sans Regular/Medium
- Labels: DM Sans 600 uppercase tracking-widest

## Idea 2: "Silicon Ambition" — Dark Tech Entrepreneurship ✅ SELECTED

**Design Movement:** Dark Mode Tech Startup meets Professional Fraternity

**Core Principles:**

1. Dark charcoal/near-black backgrounds with electric green accents (nods to SEP's existing brand)
2. Bold condensed sans-serif (Barlow Condensed) for impact headings
3. Clean grid-based layouts with sharp edges — no excessive rounding
4. High-contrast white text on dark backgrounds for readability

**Color Philosophy:**

- Primary Background: `#0D0D0D` (near black)
- Secondary Background: `#1A1A1A` (dark card)
- Accent Green: `#22C55E` (electric green — matching SEP's brand)
- Accent Gold: `#F59E0B` (warm gold for highlights)
- Text: Pure White `#FFFFFF`
- Muted Text: `#A0A0A0`
  Emotional intent: Ambitious, modern, entrepreneurial — aligned with SEP's startup-focused identity.

**Layout Paradigm:**

- Full-viewport hero with dark overlay on group photo
- Horizontal stats ticker with large numbers
- Values section with image carousel and dark overlay text
- Full-bleed "places we're at" section with company logo grid
- Dark footer with green accent links

**Signature Elements:**

1. Green glowing borders on hover states
2. Sharp rectangular buttons (no rounded corners)
3. Monospace numbers for stats

**Interaction Philosophy:**

- Hover: green glow/border reveal
- Buttons: sharp rectangular with green fill on hover
- Smooth scroll with section reveals

**Animation:**

- Hero: text slides in from left
- Stats: count-up on scroll
- Values carousel: auto-advance with manual controls
- Company logos: infinite horizontal scroll ticker

**Typography System:**

- Display: Barlow Condensed ExtraBold (hero headings)
- Subheadings: Barlow SemiBold
- Body: Inter Regular
- Labels: Inter 600 uppercase

## Idea 3: "Gaucho Spirit" — Bold Editorial

**Design Movement:** Editorial Magazine meets University Spirit

**Core Principles:**

1. UCSB colors (navy blue and gold) as primary palette
2. Large editorial typography with dramatic scale contrast
3. Asymmetric grid layouts inspired by magazine design
4. Textured backgrounds with subtle grain

**Color Philosophy:**

- Primary: UCSB Navy `#003660`
- Accent: UCSB Gold `#FEBC11`
- Background: Warm White `#FAFAF8`
- Dark Section: `#1C1C1E`
  Emotional intent: School pride, editorial sophistication, and professional ambition.

**Layout Paradigm:**

- Hero with large editorial headline overlapping image
- Stats in large typographic display
- Values in magazine-style pull-quote layout
- Company logos in editorial grid

**Signature Elements:**

1. UCSB gold horizontal rules
2. Large drop-cap initials
3. Navy-to-transparent gradient overlays

**Interaction Philosophy:**

- Hover: gold underline reveal
- Buttons: navy with gold border
- Scroll: editorial page-turn feel

**Animation:**

- Hero: cinematic zoom-in on background
- Stats: bold number reveal
- Sections: horizontal slide-in

**Typography System:**

- Display: Bebas Neue (hero)
- Subheadings: Libre Baskerville Bold
- Body: Source Sans Pro
